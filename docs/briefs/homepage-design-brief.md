## 1. The brand in three paragraphs

iNi ViE Hospitality is a Bali based hospitality group. Not one hotel: six lines
of business across one island. Resorts and villas, Wonderspace for restaurants
and beach clubs, Svaha Wellness for spas, Souljourn, the WeInivie membership,
and a B2B consultancy. **The homepage is a door to six worlds, not a page
selling one property.** Every section is one of those doors.

Its tagline is *"Turn Bali Into Yours."* That is possessive and intimate, and it
is the opposite of how luxury hospitality usually speaks. Four of the company's
eight published mantras are about local people, local culture, and the
environment rather than about luxury. This brand sells closeness, not distance.
Any reference that sells distance is being read for its layout only.

Three mantras translate directly into design constraints:

| Mantra | Constraint |
| --- | --- |
| *"Technology is a tool, not the hero"* | No carousel, no parallax, no showy motion. Calm and fast. |
| *"Meaningful story telling"* | Section order carries a narrative. Spacing rhythm is its visual form. |
| *"Every guest is seen, heard, and remembered"* | One primary action per section. Never five offers at once. |

## 2. The locked design system

Colour and type are final, measured against WCAG AA, and enforced by
`web/src/design/palette.test.ts`, which parses `web/src/app/globals.css` on every
test run. **Do not propose alternative palettes.** If a reference tempts a change
here, the reference is wrong.

### 2.1 Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ink` | `#1C2434` | Primary text, dark panels, footer |
| `ink-muted` | `#4A5468` | Secondary text |
| `accent` | `#FF8737` | Button fills and markers only. Never text on a light surface |
| `accent-hover` | `#F46100` | Hover and pressed |
| `gold` | `#C9A779` | Rules, dividers, markers. Never text on a light surface |
| `gold-dark` | `#8E6A39` | The only gold that may be text on a light surface. Used for eyebrows |
| `surface` | `#FFFFFF` | Card backgrounds |
| `surface-alt` | `#F7F7F5` | Alternating section backgrounds |
| `border` | `#E4E6EA` | Separators only, never text |
| `muted` | `#AAB1BB` | Placeholder and disabled only. Never carries meaning |
| `on-accent` | `#1C2434` | Text on an accent fill. This is `ink`, not white |

White on `accent` measures 2.39 to 1 and fails AA, which is why `on-accent`
resolves to ink at 6.49 to 1. Gold on a light surface measures 2.26 to 1, which
is why eyebrows use `gold-dark` at 4.92 to 1. Gold is legible as text only on a
dark panel, at 6.87 to 1.

**Accent discipline.** Orange appears on the primary button, the active
navigation item, and small markers such as a rating star. It is never a section
background, a large fill, or a decorative shape. When a section needs emphasis it
uses an `ink` dark panel, not orange.

### 2.2 Type

Poppins Medium for headings, Inter for body and UI. Both self hosted through
`next/font/google`.

| Role | Mobile | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1, hero | 34px / 40 | 46px / 52 |
| H2, section | 28px / 36 | 34px / 42 |
| H3, card title | 18px / 26 | 20px / 28 |
| Body | 15px / 24 | 16px / 26 |
| Small, labels | 13px / 18 | 13px / 18 |
| Eyebrow | 12px / 16, uppercase, 0.08em tracking | same |

Paragraphs cap at roughly 65 characters. The cap sits on the text container, not
the section, so a section stays full width while its copy stays readable.

### 2.3 Spacing, radius, elevation, motion

| Context | Mobile | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section vertical padding | 64px | 96px |
| Container max width | - | 1280px |
| Container side padding | 20px | 40px |
| Grid gap between cards | 20px | 32px |
| Heading to body | 12px | 16px |
| Section header to content | 32px | 48px |

Radius: cards and card images 12px, buttons and inputs 8px, full round for
avatars and icon buttons only.

Elevation: two levels. A soft low opacity shadow at rest, slightly larger and
darker on card hover. No heavy or coloured shadows. Depth comes from imagery and
spacing.

Motion: 200ms, ease-out, on hover, focus, colour and transform. Scroll entrance
is a fade plus an upward translate of at most 12px. All of it disabled under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## 4. Section by section

The order below is fixed by PRD ch. 6.1. Content marked **real** is taken from
production or from the committed seed data and must be used verbatim rather than
replaced with placeholder text.

### 4.1 Header and navigation

Sticky. Transparent over the hero, solid on scroll, drawer below 1024px.

Navigation is grouped by guest intent, not by company structure. Six items, no
more, and no mega menu:

    Stay · Dine · Wellness · Offers · Membership · About

Right of the nav, one primary button: **Book Now**.

Production runs twelve paths in its header: seven nested primary items plus five
secondary ones. That is the single largest thing this redesign fixes. The B2B
consultancy lines, Consultant, DP+ and DP Construction, move to the footer. Blog,
Mantras, Sustainability and Why Choose Us sit under About.

The transparent state needs a treatment that keeps white text legible over a
photograph. A thin top down gradient scrim, not a full dark overlay that kills
the image. The active item is marked with an accent rule or underline while its
label stays ink, because accent is never text.

The drawer needs a visible close control, touch targets at least 44 by 44 pixels
on both axes, and the same item order as desktop.

### 4.2 Hero

One large image, a heading, a subheading, and a single primary CTA. **No
carousel**, so the largest contentful paint stays cheap. Height 70vh mobile,
75vh tablet, 85vh desktop.

The tagline *"Turn Bali Into Yours"* belongs to the membership section in
production, so the hero needs its own line. Two starting points, not final copy:

- *"One island. Six ways to belong to it."*
- *"Stays, tables and rituals across Bali, held to eight mantras."*

Photography leads here more than anywhere else on the page. Copy supports the
image, never the other way round.

### 4.3 Featured Properties, the dynamic section

**This is what the technical test is actually assessing.** It renders from the
Laravel API, not from static content.

Composition: eyebrow *"Stay With Us"*, heading *"Featured property for you"*, a
one to two sentence intro, the card grid, then a secondary *"View all
properties"* link. On desktop that link may sit right aligned on the heading row.
On mobile it moves below the content, because a right aligned link beside a
wrapped heading looks broken at narrow widths.

The API returns three properties by default. **Real** seed data, in sort order:

| Title | Category | Location | Rating | From, IDR per night |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leedon Villa Seminyak | Villa | Seminyak, Bali | 4.8 | 3,200,000 |
| Ajowa Resort | Resort | Nusa Dua, Bali | 4.6 | 2,400,000 |
| La Mewali Resort | Resort | Uluwatu, Bali | 4.9 | 4,100,000 |

Real excerpts, clamped to three lines:

- *"A walled garden villa two streets back from Petitenget beach, with a private pool and a full kitchen."*
- *"Sixty rooms around a central lagoon pool, a short walk from the calm water of the southern reef."*
- *"Cliffside suites above the break at Bingin, with an open air restaurant facing the sunset."*

Card treatment, per DESIGN-SYSTEM ch. 6.1:

| Element | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Image | 4:3, `object-cover`, 12px radius, scales to 1.04 on card hover with overflow clipped |
| Category badge | Top left over the image, `surface` at high opacity, `ink` text, small label |
| Rating | Top right of the content area, a `gold` star and one decimal, small size |
| Title | H3, `ink`, clamped to 2 lines, never truncated mid word |
| Location | Small, `ink-muted`, preceded by a 16px pin icon |
| Description | Body, `ink-muted`, clamped to 3 lines |
| Price | Amount in `ink` medium, the "per night" qualifier in `ink-muted` |
| Button | Full width on mobile, auto on desktop, `accent` fill with `ink` text |
| Card | `surface`, 1px `border`, 12px radius, rest elevation, raised on hover |

**Equal height is a rule.** Cards in a row match height regardless of content
length, achieved by clamping title and description and pinning the button to the
bottom, never by fixing a card height.

Three card variants must be drawn, because all three occur in the real data:

1. **Full.** Rating and price both present. Leedon Villa Seminyak.
2. **No rating.** The rating is omitted entirely, never rendered as zero or as
   an empty star.
3. **No price, inert CTA.** The whole price row is omitted, and the button
   renders visually muted and non interactive rather than linking nowhere. Svaha
   Retreat Ubud is the real row with both fields null.

Four more states belong to this section and are worth one small artboard each or
a note on artboard 1:

- **Loading.** Three skeleton cards whose dimensions match the real card
  exactly, image ratio and clamped line counts included. A skeleton with
  different dimensions causes the layout shift it exists to prevent.
- **Six cards.** The grid must tolerate up to six without layout damage.
- **Empty.** When no published properties exist the whole section disappears,
  heading included, leaving no gap.
- **API unreachable.** A quiet fallback, with the rest of the homepage still
  rendering normally.

### 4.4 The Culinary Journey

Three restaurant cards, static content. **Real** venues from production: Norii
Seminyak (Japanese), Riserva Steakhouse (Ubud), Terra Verte (Mediterranean).
Production's line for this section is *"Opening a new chapter in refined dining
experience."*

Visually lighter than the property card. These are not bookable objects on this
page, so they do not need a price row or a rating.

### 4.5 Wellness Harmony Escape

Three spa cards. **Real** venues: Svaha Spa Ajowa, Svaha Spa La Mewali, Svaha
Spa Bisma. Production's line is *"Find serenity in soulful rituals made to
restore."*

Same card weight as Culinary, so the two sections read as a pair rather than as
two unrelated treatments.

### 4.6 WeInivie Membership

**A dark `ink` panel.** This is where accent discipline is demonstrated: the
section that needs the most emphasis on the page gets it from an ink ground, not
from orange. Gold is legible as text here.

Four benefits and one CTA. Production's line is *"Turn Bali Into Yours. Make
Every Journey More Rewarding."*

### 4.7 Our Story

Narrative with four subsections. The natural four are groupings of the eight
mantras, which keeps the section true to the brand rather than generic
about-us copy:

| Subsection | Mantras it carries |
| --- | --- |
| People | Empowering Local People, Intentional Personalization |
| Culture | Respect Local Culture, Meaningful Story Telling |
| Nature | Responsible to Mother Earth, Sustainability Impact |
| Craft | Technology Adaptation, Pioneer Concept |

Production buries Mantras and Sustainability in a secondary menu. Surfacing them
here is deliberate.

### 4.8 Our Special Offers

A promotional banner grid. One column mobile, two tablet, three desktop with the
first item spanning two columns.

### 4.9 What's New

Three latest article cards, trimmed from production's six so the page does not
run too long.

### 4.10 Featured In

A row of media logos in greyscale. Production carries nine.

### 4.11 FAQ

An accordion, keyboard operable. Production carries eight question and answer
pairs.

## 5. Responsive rules

| Component | Mobile | Tablet | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property grid | 1 column | 2 columns | 3 columns |
| Culinary and wellness grids | 1 column | 2 columns | 3 columns |
| Special offers | 1 column | 2 columns | 3 columns, first spanning 2 |
| Navigation | Drawer | Drawer | Inline |
| Footer columns | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| Hero height | 70vh | 75vh | 85vh |

Design mobile first. Every rule is a minimum width, never a maximum.

Non negotiable: no horizontal scroll from 320px upward; touch targets at least
44 by 44 pixels on mobile on both axes; navigation collapses to a drawer below
1024px with a focus trap and Escape to close; no clipped or overflowing text on
the longest title in the seed data.

## 7. What to avoid

- All caps headlines.
- Mega menus or nested dropdowns in the header.
- Cream or sand palettes. Colour is closed.
- Orange as a background, a large fill, or a decorative shape.
- A hero carousel, parallax, or scroll jacking.
- Purple to blue gradients, accent rails on rounded cards, emoji as section
  markers, everything centred. These are generator defaults rather than
  decisions.
- Oversized social icons in the footer.
- Placeholder copy. Every string above is real and available.

## 8. Acceptance checks

Derived from PRD ch. 9 and DESIGN-SYSTEM ch. 7.3. A design that fails one of
these is not ready to implement.

1. No horizontal scroll at any width from 320px upward.
2. Touch targets at least 44 by 44 pixels on mobile, both axes.
3. Navigation collapses to a drawer below 1024px.
4. Every text and background pairing meets WCAG AA, 4.5 to 1 for body and 3 to 1
   for large text.
5. Accent appears only on buttons, the active nav item, and small markers.
6. Property cards in a row are equal height with the real seed content.
7. The rating omits cleanly when absent, and the price row omits entirely when
   absent, with the CTA inert rather than broken.
8. All six footer departments remain intact and legible at 375px.
9. Verified at 375px, 768px and 1440px.
